About the company

Practical scrap solutions for industrial buyers

Established in 2020, AR INDUSTRIES works in the iron and steel scrap trade. The business sources scrap materials, undertakes preparation such as sorting and cleaning where appropriate, and supplies materials to larger industrial customers according to their requirements.

Our focus is on clear communication, responsible material handling and dependable coordination from enquiry through weighing and payment.
